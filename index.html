Project / Audio

Fourth Ground

2026 · independent · Nicholas Taylor

Description

This was the final project we made for MAT 111MC, which for me was a deep, dark production. All the sounds come from Ryan's sound library (klotho) that he made. Then, using Google Colab, we used those sounds and coded arrangements of those sounds, and did some mixing in the Colab Notebook itself. I used a synth that Ryan provided outside of the sound library, and with that, created the dark, low, spacey drone. Then I grabbed the sounds that I arranged in the Colab Notebook as samples, and then made the full piece in FL Studio. It is fully produced, engineered, mixed, and mastered by me. I took inspiration from other producers like Popilization, ssongbirdssing, orchead420, and it's a style of production I have never explored before, which is why I wanted to make it.

I'll Call On Your Name

2026 · independent · Nicholas Taylor

Description

This track is a purely instrumental track that I made in FL studio, arranged, engineered, mixed, and mastered by me. The main melody is a instrumental guitar from the song "and the rest..." by yoshi douglas, where it is reversed, different filters, which makes the original sample unrecognizable. This is my first fully produced song with a sampled element that persists through the song, and where I sample other movie vocals. The old TV sound effects that are scattered throughout give this beat a blippy, retro atmosphere.

Project / Video

Coward Music Video

2024 · independent · Nicholas Taylor

Description

"Coward" is a song that I wrote the lyrics to, performed, mixed, and mastered by me in FL Studio. The guitar instrumental was made by a different producer. The song was made in a darker place for me, and the aesthetic I built for this is a representation of that. The music video consists of clips I took around the Isla Vista area because of how foggy the coast can get. I edited the video in Cap Cut. I made this solely for me; it means a lot to me and was very representative of my mental health and where I was in life. This song's alternate title was "Diary Entry Number 3" to illustrate the personal nature of this song.

Droplet (w/ fubar & tgwog)

Description

This beat was a collaberatation with fubar and tgwog, where they did the melody and I did all the percussion. In the original melody, it was very glassy and open sounding, with lots of reverb and space being used in the melody. It gave a really ambient feel, so with the percussion, I wanted to keep that feeling with wet reverb, delay, and heavy sine wave bass with fast attack. Overall it gives a very ambient and wet sound to this beat that I really like. @fubar.cfg on Instagram and @fubarcfg on Soundcloud. @realtgwog on Instagram and @tgwog on Soundcloud.

Project / Audio

MVP

Nicholas Taylor

Description

This is one of my projects that I made in my MAT 111AU class at UCSb during the summer while completing my Media Arts & Design minor. I produced everything here in Abelton, which was my first time ever creating a full song using Abelton, and I really like how this turned out. The synths at the start were inspired byice levels in diffrerent Super Mario Games, most notably in Super Mairo Galaxy for being soft, but with structure and with higher pitched notes. Then I wanted to make the main section extremely cinematic, with strings as many Nintendo compositions are, but also I was inspired by producers online to use live strings in my music, so using Spitfire Audio's BBC Orchestra Library, and it gives the piece a really emotional and cinematic element that persists throughout the song. I add horns and cymbals at certain parts to emphasize areas of the song, and then it all stops and softens up before reurning to the orignial synth. I felt as if this one one of my favorite instrumentals I've made to date. It was such a different experience and workflow and extremely different from mhy normal proudction that uses a lot of samples.

About northbound

Northbound is a gallary of phtotos in my time in Lund, Sweden from August to December of 2025. The audio provide is something I solo produced myself, and it really represents the feeling and my time in Sweden. It was very somber and soft, with a lot of ambience. There is a static TV sound to represent something familiar, but unrecognizable to everyone, which is what Sweden felt like to me. The photo gallary below is a complete representation of my time in Sweden, where it was a mix of scenic, liminal, and atmospheric in a way you can only apprciate and understand when you are there. It defined a lot about me and changed my mindset towards school and life in 2026.

The layer beneath the surface

01 / person

My name is Nicholas Taylor and I am a producer and a visual artist from Torrance, California. I have been involved with playing music since middle school, but never really fell in love with music until I got to college. I got by "foreground" on most if not all of my musical work.

02 / study

I am currently an Applied Mathematics student at University of California, Santa Barbara, and I am minoring in Media Arts & Design. Math was something that really called ttome, and in a way it allowed me to be creative and challenege my mind working in such absrtact space. I credit math for a lot of my musical progression and it is one of the most important aspects in my life.

03 / connection

Math, music, and nature all influence me so much. Going to Sweden for a semester in Fall, 2025 really highlighted how important nature was to me in my creative process. I think without that experience, not only would I understand how important nature is to me, but the other two wouldn't have developed nearly as much.
